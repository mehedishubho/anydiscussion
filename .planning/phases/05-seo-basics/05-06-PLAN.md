Fix: sonner (registry-verified, see threat model SC row) mounted dashboard-only, with success + error toasts fired from PostForm and PageForm mutations. Purpose: the content lifecycle (draft -> review -> publish) becomes operable from the UI, and every save gives unambiguous feedback.
